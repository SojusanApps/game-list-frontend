## 1. Who's responsible for your data

For Sojusan GameList (the "Service"), the data controller is **Sojusan**, an individual based in Poland, not a registered company. Contact: **<contact@sojusan.pl>**.

This policy covers the Service itself — game lists, reviews, ratings, and social features. It does **not** cover account creation, login, or password data, which are handled entirely by **Sojusan Auth**, a separate identity service. If you have questions specifically about your login credentials or session, they're best directed to Sojusan Auth.

## 2. What we collect

Through your use of the Service, we (via our backend) hold:

- **Profile data**: your username, email address (visible only to you and to staff, never to other users), gender, avatar (see Gravatar below), and your account's join/activity timestamps.
- **Activity data**: your game lists, ratings, reviews, list and collection notes, and translation suggestions.
- **Social data**: your friends list and related activity shown to you and, where public, to others.
- **Moderation data**: reports you file or that are filed against your content, and your warning count (visible only to you and to staff).

We don't collect a birthdate, and we don't run any analytics or tracking scripts — there's nothing beyond what's listed above.

## 3. Why we process it

We process this data because it's necessary to provide the Service you've signed up for — maintaining your lists, showing your reviews, running the moderation system, and connecting you with friends. Where it isn't strictly necessary for the contract (for example, showing your username to other users), we rely on our legitimate interest in running a functioning community platform, balanced against your rights.

## 4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data, ever. We share data only with the services needed to run the Service:

- **IGDB** — supplies game, company, and cover-art data. No personal data is sent to IGDB.
- **Gravatar** (Automattic) — if you use an avatar, your email address is sent directly to Gravatar to manage it.
- **Steam** — only if you use the optional library-import feature: the Steam Profile ID you enter is sent, via our backend, to Steam's API to fetch your public library.
- **Sojusan Auth** — handles your login credentials and session; see Section 1.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where that's the case, they're responsible for their own legal safeguards for that transfer.

## 5. Cookies

We don't use analytics or tracking cookies. The only cookie involved is the necessary session cookie set by Sojusan Auth to keep you signed in — it's required for the Service to work and isn't used for tracking.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If your account is **banned**, it's permanently deactivated as described in our Terms, and we retain the minimum data needed for moderation records. If you **delete your account** through Sojusan Auth, we stop processing your personal data going forward; some content or records may be retained where we have a legitimate reason to (for example, an already-resolved moderation report), consistent with data-minimization principles.

## 7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights:

- For account/login data (email, credentials): use your Sojusan Auth account console, or contact us and we'll point you there.
- For anything else covered by this policy (lists, reviews, profile data): contact **<contact@sojusan.pl>**.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Poland, the **Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO)**.

## 8. Children

The Service isn't intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your data, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: **<contact@sojusan.pl>**.
